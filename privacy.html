Privacy Policy

Once Upon a Brick Uploader · last updated 10 November 2026

The Once Upon a Brick Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the Once Upon a Brick channel to manage its own accounts on YouTube and TikTok. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to use the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API in order to:

  • upload the channel's own videos and set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish times;
  • read statistics for the channel's own videos (for example views, likes, comments and average view duration);
  • read and reply to comments on the channel's own videos.

The app does not access any other Google data, and it does not access other people's accounts.

TikTok

When the channel owner signs in with TikTok (TikTok Login Kit), the app asks for these permissions, used only for the channel's own TikTok account (@onceupon.abrick):

  • user.info.basic: the account's display name, avatar and ID, to show which account is signed in;
  • video.upload and video.publish: to send the channel's own finished videos to its TikTok account, as a draft or as a post with the caption and privacy setting the owner chooses;
  • user.info.stats and video.list: to read the account's follower count and the view, like, comment and share counts of its own videos.

Nothing is posted without the owner's action. The app does not access other TikTok users' data. See the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Privacy Policy.

How data is stored

The sign-in tokens and the video statistics are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server run by the app, and they are not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties. Summaries of the channel's own video statistics may be kept in the owner's private notes.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. Deleting the local token file removes the app's access immediately; locally stored statistics can be deleted by the owner at any time.

This website

This website counts page views and clicks on its links with GoatCounter, so the channel can see which models people open. GoatCounter sets no cookies and stores no personal data: it keeps the page, the link clicked, the referring site, and the browser, screen size and country in aggregate. Links to other sites carry utm_ tags naming Once Upon a Brick as the source.
