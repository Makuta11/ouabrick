Once Upon a Brick

Once upon a time, there was a single brick…

Once Upon a Brick makes short films of digital brick builds: characters, castles and landscapes that rise brick by brick, from the first piece to the finished model, with the sky and the story changing around them. Every build is generated and rendered on a computer.

Watch on YouTube: @once-upon-a-brick · TikTok: @onceupon.abrick

About this site

This site also documents the small tool the channel uses to upload its own videos to YouTube and TikTok and read their statistics. See the privacy policy and terms of service.
